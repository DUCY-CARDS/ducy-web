Privacy policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

Summary

Today ducy has no sign-up, so you don’t give us an email address, password or payment. To run games we keep what you do in clubs on our servers, and others can see some of it, including club staff and us.

This policy covers ducy (ducy.cards), including the website, its bots, friends tables and clubs, during the beta.

What we collect

WhatExamplesWhy
Player IDA random ID and its public key, made in your browserTo know a message came from you
Club recordsYour display name, avatar, role, chip balance, chip history, last seenTo run the club
Hands at club tablesActions, results and finished hands’ cardsReplays, history and disputes
All cards of club handsEvery player’s cards in each hand, folded ones too, with player IDsTo check for cheating automatically. Nobody at the club, its owner included, can see them
Club chat and help requestsWhat you write in a club’s lobby or asks of its staffTo deliver it to the club
Connection dataIP address, browser type, time of requestTo deliver messages and stop abuse
Error reportsThe error, page, site version, browser and device typeTo find and fix bugs

What we collect may change as ducy changes, and we may use any of it to run, protect, study and improve ducy.

What stays on your device

Some things are kept in your browser’s storage, such as your private key, your settings, your stats against bots, and the hands your browser keeps.

If you clear your browser’s data, this may be gone unless you saved a backup. We may not be able to recover a lost backup or its password.

WhoWhat they can see

We may change these providers or add others. We may also share data if the law requires it, to deal with abuse, to protect ducy or its users, or if who runs ducy changes.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep data for as long as we choose, and we may remove any of it at any time, with or without warning.

To clear what’s on your device, clear this site’s data in your browser. We don’t remove the data we hold about players on request, except where the law requires it.

Play money

Chips on ducy are play money with no cash value. Ducy isn’t part of anything people arrange away from it, and you shouldn’t use ducy to play for money.

Changes

We may change this policy at any time by updating it here.
